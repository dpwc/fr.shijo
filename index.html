About Me

Where Faith Meets
Understanding

Ordained as a priest in 2004 after joining the Congregation of the Mission
(Vincentian Fathers) in 1991, a lifelong commitment has been dedicated to serving people through pastoral ministry, education, and personal development. Academic qualifications include a degree in Psychology from Union Christian College, Aluva, a Doctorate in Canon Law from Spain, and a Doctorate in Positive Psychology from the United States.

Professional credentials include licensure as a Positive Psychology Practitioner through the American Board of Positive Psychology, along with certification in Artificial Intelligence from Google. The integration of psychology, spirituality, and emerging technologies continues to guide efforts toward fostering holistic well-being, resilience, and human flourishing.

Healing begins when pain is no longer hidden, but understood.
